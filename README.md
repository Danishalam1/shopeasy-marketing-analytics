# 📊 Marketing Analytics Portfolio Project – ShopEasy

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the marketing performance of **ShopEasy**, an online retail business experiencing a decline in customer engagement and conversion rates despite increased investment in marketing campaigns.

The objective of this project was to analyze business and customer data, identify key performance trends, understand customer feedback, and provide **data-driven insights and actionable recommendations** to improve marketing performance.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

ShopEasy was facing several business challenges:

* Declining customer engagement
* Fluctuating and decreased conversion rates
* Increasing marketing expenses without the expected return
* Need to better understand customer feedback and satisfaction
* Need to identify high-performing products and marketing content

The analysis focused on understanding **what is happening, identifying key patterns, and recommending what the business should do next**.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project focused on three major objectives:

1. **Increase Conversion Rates**

   * Identify factors affecting conversion performance.
   * Analyze monthly and product-level conversion trends.
   * Identify high-performing products and weaker periods.

2. **Enhance Customer Engagement**

   * Analyze views, clicks, and likes.
   * Compare different content types.
   * Identify periods of declining engagement.

3. **Improve Customer Feedback Scores**

   * Analyze customer ratings.
   * Perform sentiment analysis on customer feedback.
   * Identify positive, negative, mixed, and neutral feedback.

## 🔍 Key Findings

### 1. Conversion Rate Analysis

The overall conversion rate was **8.5%**, with significant variation across different months and products.

* January recorded the highest monthly conversion rate at **18.5%**.
* May recorded the lowest conversion rate at **4.3%**.
* December showed a recovery, reaching **10.2%**.
* High-performing products included **Kayak, Ski Boots, Surfboard, Volleyball, and Fitness Tracker**.

These findings indicate opportunities to use targeted and seasonal marketing strategies for high-performing products.

---

### 2. Customer Engagement Analysis

The analysis covered:

* Views
* Clicks
* Likes
* Content type performance

The dataset contained more than **2.98 million views**, **458K clicks**, and **73K likes**.

Customer views peaked during certain months but declined during the later part of the year, indicating a need for stronger engagement strategies.

The analysis also compared:

* Blog
* Social Media
* Video

Blog content generated particularly strong views during several periods.

## 💡 Business Insights

The analysis identified several important opportunities:

### Product-Level Opportunity

High-performing products such as **Kayaks, Ski Boots, and Baseball Gloves** can be targeted with focused marketing campaigns.

### Seasonal Opportunity

Conversion rates varied considerably by month, suggesting that marketing campaigns could be aligned with seasonal demand and historically stronger periods.

### Engagement Opportunity

The decline in views during the later part of the year indicates a need to refresh the content strategy and improve calls-to-action.

### Customer Experience Opportunity

Negative and mixed customer feedback can be analyzed to identify recurring problems and develop improvement plans.

---

## 🚀 Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were developed:

### Increase Conversion Rates

* Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products.
* Introduce seasonal promotions.
* Use personalized marketing campaigns.
* Analyze customer drop-off points in the conversion funnel.

### Improve Customer Engagement

* Experiment with interactive video content.
* Increase the use of user-generated content.
* Improve CTA placement.
* Strengthen content strategies during historically weaker engagement periods.

### Improve Customer Feedback Scores

* Create a continuous customer feedback loop.
* Analyze negative and mixed reviews to identify recurring issues.
* Develop action plans to address customer concerns.
* Follow up with dissatisfied customers where appropriate.
* Work toward improving the average customer rating closer to the **4.0 target**.
