## 🧩 Business Problem

ShopEasy, an online retail business, was experiencing declining customer engagement and falling conversion rates despite increased investment in marketing campaigns. This project analyzes campaign performance, customer engagement, and customer feedback data to identify the root causes and recommend improvements.

1. **Increase Conversion Rates** — Identify where visitors drop off in the funnel and recommend improvements.
2. **Enhance Customer Engagement** — Determine which content types drive the highest engagement.
3. **Improve Customer Feedback Scores** — Identify recurring themes in reviews to guide product/service improvements.
